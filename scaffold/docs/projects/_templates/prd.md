# {Project Title} — PRD

> Drafted: YYYY-MM-DD
> Status: in-progress | shipped | paused

## Problem statement

What is unsettled, missing or due, from the point of view of the person it
concerns. Two or three sentences.

## Solution

What will be true once this project is finished: what exists, what was sent,
what is settled.

## User stories

A numbered list of everything that has to come out of the project. Each
story uses the form:

1. As a **<who it is for>**, I want **<what>**, so that **<benefit>**.
2. ...

Stories that can be finished on their own become individual
`.agents/issues/NNNN-...md` files.

## Decisions

Choices made up-front. Each one states the tradeoff:

- **Decision 1.** What was picked, and why over the alternative.
- **Decision 2.** ...

## Evidence

- What shows that a story is finished? (A signed document, a confirmation, a
  file in a named folder.)
- Where do the results live, and how are files named?
- Which deadlines are fixed from outside?

## Out of scope

Explicit boundaries. Things that look related but are not part of this
project. Each entry says *why* it was left out, so nobody argues it again.

## Further notes

- Who is involved, and how they are reached.
- Open questions from the kickoff that were answered later; superseded
  answers are removed (the PRD reflects current intent).
