Brand Kit

Calm, neutral and dense — a tool you live in next to your editor. Cool grays, one violet accent, Inter for the interface and JetBrains Mono for ids and paths. Everything here is rendered from src/styles.css: change a token there and this page changes with it.

Rules

  • Tokens only. Colors come from the custom properties below; never hard-code one. Mix with color-mix(in oklch, var(--token) N%, …). Both themes must pass pnpm contrast.
  • One accent. Violet marks the primary action, the current selection, focus and links. Tones (orange, blue, green, red, pink, amber, teal) identify projects and labels; status colors belong to status icons only. Neither is decoration.
  • Gray does the work. Hierarchy comes from the four text levels and the surface steps, not from borders or color. Borders are 1px and quiet; shadows only lift things that float (menus, dialogs, panels).
  • Type. Inter everywhere: 13px UI, 12px meta, 14–15px reading, 600 for titles. Mono only for ids, paths, keys and code. No uppercase body text; small section labels may be uppercase at 11px.
  • Quiet by default. Secondary actions appear on hover or in the right-click menu; badges appear only when they say something (waiting, reviewed, deployed).
  • Plain words, sentence case. "Needs you", "Copy agent prompt", "Changed on disk". Say what happens, not how it works. No exclamation marks.
  • Keyboard first. Every action has a ⌘K command; plain-key shortcuts live in src/shortcuts.ts so Help lists them. Focus is always visible (the accent ring).
  • Components. Dialogs use BaseDialog, choices Segmented, menus the shared Menu; status, assignee, labels and projects use their primitive components. Confirmations are inline ("Remove… → Remove / Keep"), never confirm().
  • Motion. 120 ms for hover and press, 200 ms for panels and menus, var(--ease). Nothing bounces.
