## User story

As a **maintainer**, I want the rail to be the one place to filter the feed and reach every destination, with icons and a collapsed mode, so that navigation is fast and the board gets room.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Feed views Inbox, Needs you, Sessions, Issues with counts; the feed's chip row is gone
- [x] Workspace section: All issues (list view), Projects, Specs; then Views and Projects
- [x] Every entry has an icon; the rail collapses to icons and expands again, remembered
- [x] Footer: Help, Settings; the theme toggle moves into Settings
- [x] Specs § 2 and § 13 updated; e2e cover the rail views and collapse

## Out of scope

- Resizing beyond the existing rail drag (#0048)
